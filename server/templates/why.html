Why am I seeing this?

An app you installed uses barkit to show a small message next to your clock. The developer of that app chose what to show; barkit checks that the developer is verified and that every message is signed and labelled.

How to control it

  • Right-click the widget and choose Hide for 12 hours.
  • Check the app's own settings; many let you turn the widget off.
  • Uninstalling every app that uses barkit removes the widget completely.

barkit never collects personal information. Apps only see how many times their message was shown and clicked.
